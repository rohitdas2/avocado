Core Features

Everything the paid apps do.
Without the paywall.

Avocado gives you pro-level nutrition tools with a beautiful dark-mode interface — powered by real science, not marketing.

🎯

Adaptive Maintenance Calories

Your TDEE updates automatically based on your actual weight trends and intake — not just a formula. Gets smarter every day you log.

💊

Micronutrient Deficiency Alerts

Tracks 25 vitamins & minerals against RDA targets. Color-coded dashboard shows gaps instantly with food suggestions to fix them.

📷

Barcode Scanner

Scan any packaged food to instantly log nutrition data. Powered by Open Food Facts — the world's largest open food database.

❤️

Apple Health Integration

Steps, active energy, and weight sync from Apple Health. Your maintenance calories adjust based on real activity data.

📊

Weight Trend Analysis

EMA-smoothed weight trends cut through water weight noise to show your real trajectory. Confidence scoring rates data quality.

🔍

USDA Food Database

Search 380,000+ foods from the USDA FoodData Central database. Full nutrition profiles with macros and micronutrients.

Insights Tab

Your body,
decoded.

The Insights tab combines HealthKit activity data, weight history, and food logs to calculate your real maintenance calories — not a generic estimate. It gets more accurate with every day you log.
